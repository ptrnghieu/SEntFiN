# Calibrating FiQA's score thresholds to SEntFiN's labels

_Generated by `python -m absa_data calibrate`._

SEntFiN gives hard classes; FiQA gives scores in [-1, 1] that are cut into classes at thresholds. The FinBen thresholds (±0.1) make only 7.8 % of FiQA neutral against 38 % of SEntFiN, so the two datasets' "neutral" mean different things. An LLM taught SEntFiN's conventions with 21 SEntFiN *train* examples is used as a bridge annotator: its agreement with SEntFiN gold is checked on SEntFiN *valid*, then the FiQA thresholds that best reproduce its FiQA judgements are fitted. FiQA labels are still computed from the human scores.

## 4. Thresholds

| thresholds | neg_below | pos_from | agreement with bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| FinBen | -0.1 | 0.1 | 73.8% |
| best fit | -0.01 | 0.22 | 77.7% |
| **chosen** | **-0.1** | **0.22** | 77.3% |


Bootstrap 95 % intervals of the best fit: neg_below [-0.161, 0.0], pos_from [0.179, 0.28]. Rule: a FinBen threshold is kept unless its interval excludes it (the agreement surface is flat over wide ranges, so a marginally better point estimate is not evidence). The build uses the chosen thresholds for `polarity` / `label` and the soft labels. Thresholds are fitted on FiQA train + valid (939 pairs); on the held-out FiQA test (234 pairs) the chosen thresholds agree with the bridge on 80.8% vs 78.2% for FinBen.
